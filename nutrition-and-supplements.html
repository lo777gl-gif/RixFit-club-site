Supplements

Supplements are a great way to boost recovery and help build muscle for anyone who has a hard time filling out the necessary elements. They are all varied and are used in different occasions. For example, if you quickly get tired, consider using pre-workout supplement.

Whey Protein
A protein supplement used in case a person lacks in proteins in his diet to fill out the rest of the intake. Helps in recovery.
Creatine
A supplement used for better performance during your gym sessions, especially useful for high-intensity training. It participates in ATP regeneration which will boost your energy in high amounts.
Pre-Workout
While Creatine helps with a long-term strength and helps with muscle growth itself, your body still needs something to build and utilize it. That's where Pre-Workout comes in, with an immediate source of energy in forms of caffeine and quick carbs.

More general nutrition information can be found from the WHO .

Before Supplement Use

  1. Know exactly what do you need from your training. Whether it is for strength, flexibility or hypertrophy.
  2. Go see your nutriologist, make sure that you can handle it.
  3. Use the supplement together with your meals and check instructions.

Nutrition Warning

Supplements are great as its name suggests in case you have problem satisfying your intake with just your diet. But by no means should it replace your regular meals.

RixFit - Services

RixFit offers countless of different services. Ranging from the gym to spa. Gym includes in itself professionals in the face of coaches who are proficient in bodybuilding, powerlifting and whatnot. Saunas of all kinds: Turkish hammam, russian vaporarium and infrared sauna with salt block. All of which contributes to overall health, relaxation and well-being. Then there are pools. One for kids and one for adults. In each there are master of sports who will teach both to learn and to compete. And as a finishing touch there is a summer terrace, which completely makes sense since RixFit Fitness & Spa also poses itself to be a 'wellness-center'.

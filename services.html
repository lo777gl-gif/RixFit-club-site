RixFit - Services

Jump to: Swimming Pool Sauna Summer Terrace

RixFit Fitness & offers countless of different services. Ranging from the gym to spa. Gym includes in itself professionals in the face of coaches who are proficient in bodybuilding, powerlifting and whatnot. Saunas of all kinds: Turkish hammam, russian vaporarium and infrared sauna with salt block. All of which contributes to overall health, relaxation and well-being. Then there are pools. One for kids and one for adults. In each there are master of sports who will teach both to learn and to compete. And as a finishing touch there is a summer terrace, which completely makes sense since RixFit Fitness & Spa also poses itself to be a 'wellness-center'.

Relaxation Areas

Swimming Pool

Pool in the RixFit Fitness provides both relaxation and activity. It is separated into two, one for adults and another for kids. Since the swimming pool is also used as a place for exercise, there are also classes with capable and qualified instructors. After exercising, visitors can use the pool and take some time in enjoying the warm water. There are also, sometimes, competitions for kids within the gym.

Sauna

The sauna area is designed to be a recovery place, with different sections, such as hammam and vaporarium. Warm environment which helps visitors unwind, spending their time in a healthy relaxing routine. While a traditional, steam-based sauna is intense, there is also an infrared sauna which is a good alternative and more effective. Together with swimming pool, that gives a complete contribution to the wellness of the gym-goers.

Summer Terrace

With one and only summer terrace in Astana located in the Khan-Shatyr, RixFit Fitness offers it to its visitors to sunbathe on a deck chair. Open during warm weather, gym-goers can have a great view on Astana along with enjoying quite time after a gym session.

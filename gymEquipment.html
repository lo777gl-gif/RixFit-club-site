Start with the equipment you recognise

A public description of RixFit lists Technogym equipment, cardio machines, free weights, and a separate pool area. The list below is a guide for identifying the equipment; it is not a substitute for an in-person induction from a trainer. Ask a staff member to demonstrate any unfamiliar movement, especially if you have an injury or medical restriction. In a safe session, the goal is controlled technique, not the heaviest possible load.

Before your first set

A simple order for a safe start

  1. Check the machine label and ask a trainer if the purpose is unclear.
  2. Adjust the seat, pad, or handle so your starting position is stable.
  3. Choose a light load and complete a slow test repetition.
  4. Keep the movement controlled and stop if you feel sharp pain.
  5. Return the load and wipe the contact points after use.

Equipment terms

Words used in the guide

Cardio
Exercise that keeps the body moving continuously to train endurance.
Resistance
The load that the muscles work against, such as a plate, cable, or dumbbell.
Range of motion
The controlled distance a joint travels during an exercise.

Important: equipment names can differ between machines. Use the label on the machine and the trainer’s instruction as your final reference.

Do not start a machine you have not been shown how to use. A trainer can help you set the correct position for your body.

An exercise plan may record repetitions as 3 × 10; the small number is a count, not a measurement of the machine. Use reps and ROM consistently in your notes.

Hydration matters during training: water is written as H2O. A training note can label a second set as the 2nd set after you confirm the plan with a trainer.

Club Rules & Regulations

1. Dress Code & Hygiene

  • Clean athletic shoes (sneakers) and appropriate workout attire are strictly required in all training areas.
  • Using an individual sweat towel on exercise equipment and workout benches is mandatory.
  • Outdoor footwear, open-toed shoes (slides, sandals), or barefoot training are not permitted in the gym.

2. Gym Floor Etiquette

  • Return all equipment to its designated racks immediately: dumbbells by weight, plates by size, and barbells to stands.
  • Do not drop weights, dumbbells, or barbells onto bare floors or non-lifting platforms.
  • Wipe down machine touchpoints and leather pads with disinfectant wipes after completing your sets.
  • Share equipment between sets during peak hours and avoid occupying stations solely to use personal mobile devices.

3. Health & Safety

  • Assess workout intensity according to your physical capabilities; use spotters or ask an on-duty instructor when lifting heavy loads.
  • Access to facilities is strictly prohibited for individuals under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or showing symptoms of infectious illnesses.
  • In case of an injury or sudden health issue, notify the floor supervisor or front desk staff immediately.

4. Locker Rooms & Belongings

  • Store personal items in locked lockers during your visit. The club is not responsible for unattended valuables.
  • Lockers must be cleared before the club closes; overnight storage is not allowed unless a long-term locker rental is active.
  • Photography and video recording inside locker rooms and shower areas are strictly prohibited.

5. Membership & Access Policy

  • Entry requires presenting a valid personal membership ID card. Passes are non-transferable.
  • Workouts must conclude, and training zones must be vacated at least 15 minutes before the facility's closing time.
  • Members are limited to one guest pass per month*.

* Guest passes must be arranged with the front desk at least 24 hours in advance.

Swimming Pool Rules & Regulations

Pool water (H2O) is tested twice daily for chlorine and pH levels.

  1. Entry into the pool area is strictly permitted only in pool slides or flip-flops.
  2. Access to the pool is permitted only in swimwear or swim trunks (no other clothing is allowed).
  3. A swim cap is mandatory – sauna hats or disposable plastic caps are strictly prohibited.
  4. Taking a shower is mandatory before entering the pool and after visiting the sauna.
  5. Bringing food, beverages, or chewing gum into the pool area is strictly forbidden.
  6. Children are only allowed in the swimming pool under direct adult supervision.
  7. Diving or jumping into the pool from the edge is strictly prohibited.
  8. In the event of a rule violation, the management reserves the right to deny access to the pool.

Glossary of Terms

Peak hours
06:00-09:00 and 18:00-21:00, when the gym floor is busiest.
Long-term locker rental
A paid monthly subscription that lets a member keep the same locker overnight instead of clearing it daily.
